# 🌳 The Biblical Family Tree · From Adam to Jesus

A comprehensive, visually immersive website tracing the complete biblical genealogy from Creation to Christ. Every patriarch, every generation, every story explained in rich detail with historical context, theological significance, and practical insights.

## ✨ Features

- **Complete Genealogy:** Every major figure from Adam through Jesus Christ, covering approximately 4,000 years of biblical history
- **20 Detailed Chapters:** Adam & Eve, Cain & Abel, Pre-Flood Patriarchs, Noah, Shem/Ham/Japheth, Abraham, Ishmael & Isaac, Jacob & Esau, The Twelve Sons, Joseph's Story, Moses & Exodus, Joshua, The Judges, The Kings, The Prophets, Exile & Return, and Jesus Christ
- **Rich Historical Context:** Archaeological insights, ancient Near Eastern cultural backgrounds, Hebrew word studies, and scholarly perspectives
- **Theological Depth:** Covenant promises, Messianic prophecies, typology, and the unified narrative of Scripture
- **Family Relationships Explained:** Wives, children, birth orders, tribal inheritances, and the significance of each person in God's redemptive plan
- **Emerald Forest Theme:** Deep green and gold color palette with organic, ancient aesthetic — completely distinct design language
- **Sticky Sidebar Navigation:** Easy access to all 20+ sections with active link tracking
- **Fully Responsive:** Beautiful on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Zero Dependencies:** Pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks or libraries required
- **Accessible:** Keyboard navigation, semantic HTML, ARIA labels, and print-friendly styles

## 📖 Content Overview

### The Story Begins
The genealogy starts with **Adam and Eve** in the Garden of Eden, covering their creation, the Fall, life outside Eden, and the promise of a redeemer (Genesis 3:15 — the Protoevangelium).

### The First Brothers
**Cain and Abel** — the first murder, the mark of Cain, and the cultural developments of Cain's descendants. **Seth** — the appointed seed through whom the godly line continues.

### Ten Generations Before the Flood
Detailed profiles of every patriarch from Adam to Noah, including their ages, name meanings, and the extraordinary case of **Enoch**, who "walked with God" and was taken without dying.

### The Flood and New Beginning
**Noah's** ark, the worldwide flood, the Noahic Covenant with the rainbow as its sign, and the repopulation of the earth through Noah's three sons.

### The Patriarchs
**Abraham's** call, covenant, and testing. **Isaac's** marriage to Rebekah. **Jacob's** deception, ladder dream, wrestling with God, and name change to Israel. **Joseph's** journey from slave to ruler of Egypt.

### The Twelve Tribes
Complete profiles of all twelve sons of Jacob — their mothers, name meanings, tribal characteristics, and prophetic blessings. Special attention to **Judah** (the Messianic line) and **Joseph** (the double inheritance through Ephraim and Manasseh).

### Moses to Monarchy
The Exodus, giving of the Law, wilderness wandering, conquest of Canaan under Joshua, the period of the Judges, and the establishment of the monarchy under Saul, David, and Solomon.

### Exile and Return
The division of the kingdom, the fall of Israel to Assyria and Judah to Babylon, the prophetic ministries, and the return under Persian kings.

### Jesus Christ
The two New Testament genealogies (Matthew and Luke), their differences and purposes, and how Jesus fulfills every covenant promise — the Seed of the woman, the Son of Abraham, the Lion of Judah, the Son of David.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The emerald forest theme was chosen to evoke:
- **Growth** — the family tree expanding across generations
- **Life** — the living chain of faith from Adam to Christ
- **Ancient wisdom** — deep greens suggesting timeless truth
- **Hope** — gold accents representing divine promise and fulfillment

The color palette is intentionally distinct from companion projects:
- *Parables of Jesus* — warm parchment tones
- *Kings of the Bible* — royal purple and gold
- *Biblical Family Tree* — emerald forest and gold

## 🔧 Customization

- **Colors:** Edit the CSS custom properties in the `:root` block of `style.css`
- **Typography:** Modify `--font-serif` and `--font-sans` variables
- **Content:** All genealogical content is in `index.html` within semantic `<section>` elements
- **Navigation:** Add or remove sidebar links in the `<nav class="sidebar-nav">` section
