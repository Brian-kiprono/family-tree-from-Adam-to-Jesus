About This Study

This comprehensive genealogical study traces the biblical family tree from Adam to Jesus Christ, covering approximately 4,000 years of history. It includes:

  • Every major patriarch with family relationships, life events, and spiritual significance
  • Historical and cultural context from ancient Near Eastern backgrounds
  • Theological insights showing how each generation carried forward God's covenant promises
  • An interactive visual diagram where you can see the entire tree at once and hover over names for details
  • Easy navigation through the sidebar or quick-link cards

Whether you're studying for personal devotion, teaching preparation, or historical interest, this resource provides both breadth and depth.
